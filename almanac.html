I · Luna hac nocte tonight

Reckoned at 21 h 30, Europe/Rome. The seal is turned as the eye sees it from the station — the bright limb at its true position angle, the zenith upward; when the moon is below the horizon it is shown as it will stand at meridian passage. Places from a low-precision lunar theory, to a few tenths of a degree; risings to a few minutes. Next syzygies in mean lunations of 29 d 12 h 44 m 02.9 s.
