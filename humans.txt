/* PLACIDUM */

Imprint: Placidum, self-release, Paris
Hand: Sacha Alexandre Khalifé (Shcaa, Ain°)
Correspondence: sacha@placidum.com
Station: 36° 49′ 27.2″ N, 12° 00′ 44.8″ E

/* MATTER */

Music: composed, played and recorded by human hands, all of it
Notes: written by the hand above; quotations named where they stand
Photographs: the hand above, from his negatives and files;
  artworks, film stills and covers belong to those who made them
Margins: traces cut from the same negatives (the sea, palms,
  the lamps of Paris, desert sand), stamped and inked by script,
  re-dealt once a day by the date at the station
Moon: Tobias Mayer, Tabula Selenographica, 1748,
  turned as the sky turns it, reckoned at 21 h 30, Europe/Rome
Mark: a rubbing of the drawn symbol
Vestigia Sonora: Ernst Chladni, Entdeckungen über die Theorie
  des Klanges, 1787, Tab. III, fig. 27
